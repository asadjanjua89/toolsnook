Common Questions

FAQ

Everything you might want to know about ToolsNook.

Is ToolsNook really free?
Yes — all 14 calculator tools are completely free with no limits, forever. The 6 AI writing tools offer 3 free generations per tool per day. There is no signup, no credit card, and no hidden fees.
Do you store my data?
No. Calculator results are computed entirely in your browser and never sent to any server. AI tool inputs are sent to our secure API for processing but are never stored or logged.
What currencies do your tools support?
All calculator tools support 30+ currencies including USD, GBP, EUR, PKR, INR, AED, SAR, CAD, AUD, BRL, NGN, ZAR and more. The currency converter supports live rates for all major global currencies.
How accurate are the AI tools?
Our AI tools are powered by Llama 3.3, a state-of-the-art language model. Results are high quality but should always be reviewed and edited before use. AI tools are designed to give you a strong starting point, not a final draft.
Will you be adding more tools?
Yes — we're actively building new tools. If there's a specific business tool you'd like to see, email us at hello@toolsnook.com and we'll consider it for our next release.
How do you make money if everything is free?
ToolsNook is supported by non-intrusive display advertising through Google AdSense. Ads help us keep the lights on while keeping all tools free for everyone.
