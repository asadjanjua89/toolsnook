Frequently Asked Questions

Are all ToolsNook tools really free?
Yes — all calculator tools are completely free with no limits, forever. AI writing tools offer 3 free uses per day per tool. There are no premium plans, no credit cards, and no hidden fees.
Do you store my data or calculations?
No. Calculator results are computed in your browser and never sent to any server. Image conversions happen entirely on your device — files are never uploaded. AI tool inputs are sent to a secure API for processing but are never stored or logged.
Can I request a specific tool?
Absolutely — we love tool requests. Use the contact form above with the subject "Tool Request" and describe what you need. We review all requests and build the most-requested ones regularly.
I found a bug — what should I do?
Please report it using the contact form with the subject "Bug Report". Include the tool name, what you were doing, what happened, and which browser/device you're using. We aim to fix bugs within 24 hours.
Can I embed ToolsNook tools on my website?
We're open to discussing embedding arrangements for businesses. Send us a message using the contact form with "Business" as the subject and we'll get back to you.
