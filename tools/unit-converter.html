Metric vs Imperial — A Quick History

The metric system (now formalized as the International System of Units, or SI) was developed in France in the 1790s, designed around powers of 10 for simplicity — 1 kilometer = 1,000 meters, 1 kilogram = 1,000 grams. It's used as the primary system in nearly every country in the world.

The imperial system, and its close relative the US customary system, evolved from older English units of measurement — feet, inches, pounds, gallons — many of which trace back centuries and were based on practical references (a foot was literally based on human foot length in some historical versions). The United States continues to use US customary units for most everyday purposes, while the UK officially uses metric but retains imperial units in common speech (miles for road distances, pints for beer, stone for body weight).

This mix is why unit conversion remains a daily necessity — international business, travel, cooking, engineering, science, and even casual conversations between people from different countries regularly require converting between these two systems.

Data Storage: Why "1 TB" Drives Show as 931 GB

This is one of the most common sources of confusion in computing — and it's caused by two competing definitions of the same prefixes.

UnitDefinitionBytesUsed By
KB (kilobyte)Decimal (SI)1,000Storage manufacturers, file sizes on macOS
KiB (kibibyte)Binary (IEC)1,024Memory (RAM), correct technical term
MB (megabyte)Decimal (SI)1,000,000Storage manufacturers
MiB (mebibyte)Binary (IEC)1,048,576RAM, correct technical term
GB (gigabyte)Decimal (SI)1,000,000,000Storage manufacturers
GiB (gibibyte)Binary (IEC)1,073,741,824RAM, correct technical term
TB (terabyte)Decimal (SI)1×10¹²Storage manufacturers
TiB (tebibyte)Binary (IEC)1.0995×10¹²RAM, correct technical term

Storage manufacturers advertise drives using the decimal definition — a "1 TB" drive contains 1,000,000,000,000 bytes. But Windows displays storage using binary math while still labeling it "GB" — so that same drive shows as roughly 931 "GB" (which is actually GiB) in Windows. The drive isn't smaller than advertised — it's a labeling inconsistency between the storage industry (decimal) and operating systems (binary, mislabeled).

💡 Quick way to remember: If you see "-ibi" in the name (kibi, mebi, gibi, tebi), it's binary (×1024). If it's just "kilo, mega, giga, tera", it's technically decimal (×1000) — even though many programs mislabel binary values this way. Temperature Conversion Formulas

Unlike most units, temperature scales don't share a common zero point — so conversions require formulas, not simple multiplication.

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
K = °C + 273.15
°C = K − 273.15
°F = (K × 9/5) − 459.67

Kelvin is the SI base unit for temperature and has no negative values — 0 K ("absolute zero") is the coldest possible temperature, equal to -273.15°C or -459.67°F. Kelvin is used in scientific and engineering contexts. Celsius is used for everyday temperatures in most of the world. Fahrenheit is primarily used in the United States.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert kilometers to miles?
Multiply kilometers by 0.621371 to get miles. Example: 10 km × 0.621371 = 6.21 miles. To go the other way, multiply miles by 1.609344. Use the Length tab above for instant results with any value.
How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?
°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. Example: 20°C × 1.8 = 36, + 32 = 68°F. To convert Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. Use the Temperature tab above for instant conversion.
What is the difference between a US gallon and a UK gallon?
A US gallon is 3.785 liters. A UK (Imperial) gallon is 4.546 liters — about 20% larger. The same difference applies to pints and fluid ounces. Always check which system a source uses, especially for fuel economy or recipe measurements.
What is the difference between KB and KiB?
KB (kilobyte) = 1,000 bytes (decimal/SI definition, used by storage manufacturers). KiB (kibibyte) = 1,024 bytes (binary/IEC definition, the correct term for what operating systems often mislabel as "KB"). This is why a "1 TB" drive shows as about 931 GB in Windows — Windows uses binary math but decimal labels.
How many tablespoons are in a cup?
There are 16 tablespoons in 1 US cup (also 48 teaspoons, or 8 fluid ounces). In metric, 1 US cup ≈ 236.6 mL and 1 tablespoon ≈ 14.8 mL. Use the Volume tab above for any custom conversion.
What is the difference between mass and weight?
Mass is the amount of matter in an object and is constant (kg, lbs). Weight is the force of gravity on that mass and varies by location (measured in newtons). In everyday language and on this converter, "weight" and "mass" are used interchangeably, which matches how kilograms and pounds are used in daily life.
How do I convert square feet to square meters?
Multiply square feet by 0.092903 to get square meters. Example: 1,000 sq ft × 0.092903 = 92.9 m². To go the other way, multiply square meters by 10.7639. This is especially useful when comparing real estate listings between countries.
Why do temperature conversions need formulas instead of simple multiplication?
Because temperature scales don't share a zero point — 0°C is 32°F, and 0 K is -273.15°C. Since the scales are offset from each other (not just scaled), conversion requires both a multiplication and an addition/subtraction step. Our converter applies the correct formula automatically for every temperature conversion.
