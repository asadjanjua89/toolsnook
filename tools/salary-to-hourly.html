How to Convert Salary to Hourly Rate

The basic formula divides your annual salary by the total hours worked per year. The total hours are calculated from your weekly hours and working weeks.

Hourly Rate = Annual Salary ÷ (Hours per Week × Weeks per Year)

For a standard 40-hour work week with 52 weeks per year: Annual Salary ÷ 2,080 = Hourly Rate

Why Vacation Days Matter

If you have paid vacation, your effective hourly rate stays the same since you're still paid. But if you're a freelancer calculating what to charge clients, subtract your vacation days to find how many billable hours you actually work.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many working hours are in a year?
A standard full-time employee works 40 hours per week × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours per year. Accounting for public holidays and vacation reduces this to around 1,800–1,950 hours.
How do I convert hourly to annual salary?
Multiply your hourly rate by the hours you work per week, then multiply by 52. For example: $25/hour × 40 hours × 52 weeks = $52,000/year.
