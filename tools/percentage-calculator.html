How to calculate percentages

A percentage is a number expressed as a fraction of 100. The word comes from Latin "per centum" meaning "by the hundred." Here are the most common percentage formulas:

What is X% of Y?

Result = (X / 100) × Y

Example: What is 25% of 200? → (25 / 100) × 200 = 50

X is what % of Y?

Result = (X / Y) × 100

Example: 50 is what % of 200? → (50 / 200) × 100 = 25%

Percentage change (increase or decrease)

Result = ((New - Old) / Old) × 100

Example: From 100 to 150 → ((150 - 100) / 100) × 100 = 50% increase
