How to Calculate Your Freelance Rate

Most freelancers underprice themselves because they only think about their desired income and forget about taxes, business expenses, and the fact that not all hours are billable (admin, proposals, meetings eat into your time).

Hourly Rate = Total Revenue Needed ÷ Annual Billable Hours
Total Revenue Needed = Income + Expenses + Tax Provision + Profit Buffer
What percentage of hours should be billable?
Most freelancers can realistically bill 60–75% of their working hours. The rest goes to admin, marketing, proposals, and professional development. Using 70% is a safe starting assumption.
Should I include taxes in my rate?
Absolutely. As a freelancer you're responsible for your own taxes including self-employment tax. Set aside 25–35% depending on your country. This calculator factors that in automatically.
