Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Welcome to ToolsNook ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit www.toolsnook.com.

1. Information We Collect

ToolsNook is designed to be a privacy-friendly service. We collect minimal data:

  • Usage data: We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous information about how visitors use our site, including pages visited, time spent, and general location (country/city level). This data is aggregated and cannot identify you personally.
  • Tool inputs: The data you enter into our calculator and AI tools is processed in your browser or sent to our secure API. We do not store, log, or retain any inputs you provide to our tools.
  • Cookies: We use cookies for Google Analytics and, if applicable, Google AdSense advertising. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

2. How We Use Information

  • To understand how our tools are being used so we can improve them
  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense (where applicable)
  • To monitor site performance and uptime

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to analyse traffic to our website. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous information such as how many people visit our site and which pages they view. This information is used to compile reports and help us improve the site. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Google AdSense

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting google.com/settings/ads.

5. AI Tools & Third-Party APIs

Our AI-powered writing tools send your inputs to a secure server-side API. These requests are processed in real time and are not stored or logged by us. We use Groq's API infrastructure to power these features. Groq's privacy policy is available at groq.com/privacy-policy.

6. Data Storage

ToolsNook stores a small amount of data in your browser's local storage to track your daily usage of AI tools (to enforce free usage limits). This data never leaves your device and is automatically cleared after 24 hours.

7. Children's Privacy

ToolsNook is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights

Since we collect minimal personal data, there is little to action. However, you may:

  • Disable cookies through your browser settings
  • Opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Clear your browser's local storage to remove any tool usage data

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: hello@toolsnook.com

By using ToolsNook, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.
