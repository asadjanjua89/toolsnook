Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

Overview

ToolsNook ("we", "us", "our", "the Site") operates www.toolsnook.com, a website providing free online calculators, converters, and AI-assisted writing tools. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit the Site, how it is used, and the choices available to you. By using ToolsNook, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Calculator and Tool Inputs

All calculator and converter tools on ToolsNook (such as the BMI Calculator, VAT Calculator, Discount Calculator, Word Counter, and similar tools) run entirely in your browser. Numbers, text, and files you enter into these tools are processed locally on your device and are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

AI Writing Tool Inputs

Tools that use AI (such as the AI Product Description Generator, AI Cover Letter Writer, AI Bio Generator, and AI Business Name Generator) send the text you enter to a third-party AI API provider solely to generate a response. This data is processed in real time and is not stored by ToolsNook or used to train any models. We recommend not entering sensitive personal information into AI-powered tools.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit ToolsNook, certain information may be automatically collected through Google Analytics and similar technologies, including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Approximate location (country/region, based on IP address)
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Referring website or search engine

This information is aggregated and anonymized. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Cookies

ToolsNook uses cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to function correctly, such as remembering your light/dark theme preference or recently used tools.
  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics uses cookies to understand how visitors use the Site, helping us improve content and performance.
  • Advertising cookies — Google AdSense and its partners use cookies to serve ads and measure ad performance, as described below.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies may affect some site functionality, such as remembering your theme preference.

Google AdSense & Advertising

ToolsNook uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect data about your visits to this and other websites to provide relevant advertisements.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, may show ToolsNook ads on sites across the internet based on your prior visits to this website.

For more information on how Google uses data when you use our site, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with ToolsNook. Google Analytics collects information such as how often users visit the Site, what pages they visit, and what other sites they used prior to coming to ToolsNook. Google's ability to use and share information collected by Google Analytics is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can prevent Google Analytics from recognizing you on return visits by disabling cookies in your browser, or by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Your Privacy Rights

GDPR (EU / UK / Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, and the right to object to processing or data portability. Since ToolsNook collects minimal personal data and does not require accounts, most of these requests will simply involve disabling cookies in your browser or using the opt-out tools linked above. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

CCPA (California)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion of personal information, and to opt out of the sale of personal information. ToolsNook does not sell personal information to third parties.

Children's Privacy

ToolsNook is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through ToolsNook. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
